More than a wholesale business. A relationship built over time.

Ours began with a simple belief: good business is not just about selling products; it is about earning people’s trust.

Founded in 2015 by Harish Kumar Sahu, with Hemlata as Co-Founder, Bhavesh Purse & Belts started with a vision to serve the local market with a dependable source for everyday fashion accessories and essential products. Over the years it has grown into an established wholesale supply business serving retailers, shopkeepers and businesses across Kota and nearby areas.

Behind every wholesale order is a retailer who has customers to serve and a business to run. That is why we offer variety, listen to requirements and make every business interaction worthwhile.
